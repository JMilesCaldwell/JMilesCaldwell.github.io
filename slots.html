The Piglet’s Corner Machines

Two one-armed contraptions of brass and blackened oak, wedged into the corner of the taproom. Pull a lever; should three alike align upon a row or a diagonal, the machine shall surrender its prize.

The Curio Bandit deals in the house’s own oddities, and pays roughly one pull in three.

The Lucky Dragon, older and less forgiving, is fed from The One Loot Table To Rule Them All: nigh on a thousand oddments gathered by the community. One pull in five pays.
