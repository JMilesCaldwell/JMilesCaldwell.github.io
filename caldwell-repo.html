A word of welcome…

Welcome, Traveler, to Caldwell’s Repository.

Here before you stands a compendium of maps, histories, and well-worn truths that may or may not be of use to those who travel the Evershroud Isles. These pages hold the wisdom of sages, scholars and wanderers, and it is my hope that they may shed but a sliver of light on your path in these uncertain times.

Read voraciously, for knowledge is a powerful companion. But remember, what is clear from the warmth of one's library may remain shrouded to those who walk the mists. Understanding unfolds in its own time, and even the greatest of truths can slip through the grasp of the unprepared.

May this repository serve you well on your journey, wherever the mists may take you.

J.M.C.
